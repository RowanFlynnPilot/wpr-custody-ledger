# Custody Ledger brief, Oct. 23, 2026: 23,911, down 9 from a week earlier

*Drafted by rule from the Department of Corrections' report of Oct. 23, 2026. Check it against the report before publishing.*

## Flags

Nothing flagged this week.

## Draft

Wisconsin's adult prison system held 23,911 people on Oct. 23, down 9 from a week earlier and up 377 from a year earlier, according to the Department of Corrections' weekly population report. The record is 23,931, set Oct. 9, 2026.

The department puts its design capacity at 17,860: 17,367 beds in its own prisons and centers, plus the 493 contract beds it rents, which it counts as full. That puts the system at 133.9% of capacity, 6,051 people beyond it. The highest rate in weekly records that begin in 1999 was 134.1%, on Oct. 9, 2026.

Women's prisons held 1,726 people, 176.8% of their capacity. Men's prisons were at 131.4%. Of 37 facilities with a capacity, 34 held more people than they were designed for.

County jails held 466 state prisoners on contract, up 7 from a week earlier and up 157 from a year earlier, in 12 of the 25 jails on the department's list.
