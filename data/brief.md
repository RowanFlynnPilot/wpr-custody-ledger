# Custody Ledger brief, Oct. 9, 2026: record 23,931

*Drafted by rule from the Department of Corrections' report of Oct. 9, 2026. Check it against the report before publishing.*

## Flags

- Record: 23,931 people, the seventh straight weekly record
- Crowding record: 134.1% of design capacity, the highest in weekly records that begin in 1999

## Draft

Wisconsin's adult prison system held 23,931 people on Oct. 9, up 4 from a week earlier and up 376 from a year earlier, according to the Department of Corrections' weekly population report. It is the most in weekly records that begin in 1999 and the seventh record in as many weeks.

The department puts its design capacity at 17,849: 17,366 beds in its own prisons and centers, plus the 483 contract beds it rents, which it counts as full. That puts the system at 134.1% of capacity, 6,082 people beyond it. That is the highest rate in weekly records that begin in 1999.

Women's prisons held 1,744 people, 179.6% of their capacity. Men's prisons were at 131.5%. Of 37 facilities with a capacity, 34 held more people than they were designed for.

County jails held 454 state prisoners on contract, down 2 from a week earlier and up 154 from a year earlier, in 12 of the 25 jails on the department's list.
