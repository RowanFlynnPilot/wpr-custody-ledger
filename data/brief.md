# Custody Ledger brief, Oct. 30, 2026: record 23,936

*Drafted by rule from the Department of Corrections' report of Oct. 30, 2026. Check it against the report before publishing.*

## Flags

- Record: 23,936 people, passing 23,931 set Oct. 9, 2026
- McNaughton Correctional Center: 120 people, up 3 from a week earlier, its highest count on record

## Draft

Wisconsin's adult prison system held 23,936 people on Oct. 30, up 25 from a week earlier and up 380 from a year earlier, according to the Department of Corrections' weekly population report. It is the most in weekly records that begin in 1999.

The department puts its design capacity at 17,861: 17,370 beds in its own prisons and centers, plus the 491 contract beds it rents, which it counts as full. That puts the system at 134.0% of capacity, 6,075 people beyond it. The highest rate in weekly records that begin in 1999 was 134.1%, on Oct. 9, 2026.

Women's prisons held 1,732 people, 176.7% of their capacity. Men's prisons were at 131.5%. Of 37 facilities with a capacity, 35 held more people than they were designed for.

County jails held 464 state prisoners on contract, down 2 from a week earlier and up 152 from a year earlier, in 12 of the 25 jails on the department's list.
