## Draft

Wisconsin's adult prison system held 23,935 people on Nov. 13, unchanged from a week earlier and up 444 from a year earlier, according to the Department of Corrections' weekly population report. The record is 23,936, set Oct. 30, 2026.

The department puts its design capacity at 17,865: 17,367 beds in its own prisons and centers, plus the 498 contract beds it rents, which it counts as full. That puts the system at 134.0% of capacity, 6,070 people beyond it. The highest rate in weekly records that begin in 1999 was 134.1%, on Oct. 9, 2026.

Women's prisons held 1,738 people, 177.9% of their capacity. Men's prisons were at 131.4%. Of 37 facilities with a capacity, 35 held more people than they were designed for.

County jails held 471 state prisoners on contract, up 2 from a week earlier and up 153 from a year earlier, in 12 of the 25 jails on the department's list.
