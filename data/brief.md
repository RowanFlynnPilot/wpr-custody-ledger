# Custody Ledger brief, Nov. 20, 2026: record 23,959

*Drafted by rule from the Department of Corrections' report of Nov. 20, 2026. Check it against the report before publishing.*

## Flags

- Record: 23,959 people, passing 23,936 set Oct. 30, 2026
- Lincoln County Jail: 78 state prisoners, up 2 from a week earlier, its highest count on record
- Lincoln Hills School: 62 youth, down 5 from a week earlier

## Draft

Wisconsin's adult prison system held 23,959 people on Nov. 20, up 24 from a week earlier and up 494 from a year earlier, according to the Department of Corrections' weekly population report. It is the most in weekly records that begin in 1999.

The department puts its design capacity at 17,884: 17,368 beds in its own prisons and centers, plus the 516 contract beds it rents, which it counts as full. That puts the system at 134.0% of capacity, 6,075 people beyond it. The highest rate in weekly records that begin in 1999 was 134.1%, on Oct. 9, 2026.

Women's prisons held 1,731 people, 176.8% of their capacity. Men's prisons were at 131.5%. Of 37 facilities with a capacity, 35 held more people than they were designed for.

County jails held 488 state prisoners on contract, up 17 from a week earlier and up 169 from a year earlier, in 12 of the 25 jails on the department's list.
